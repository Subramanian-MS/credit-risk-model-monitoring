# Data Attribution & Open Data Licences

The datasets used in the **Commercial Credit Risk Scorecard & Model Monitoring Platform** are sourced from official government open data portals and published under open licences.

---

## 1. U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) 7(a) Loan Program
- **Publisher:** U.S. Small Business Administration, Office of Capital Access
- **Portal:** [data.sba.gov](https://data.sba.gov/dataset/7a-504-foia)
- **Dataset:** Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) 7(a) Loan Data (FY2000–FY2009)
- **Licence:** Public Domain (United States Government Work)
- **Usage:** Used for model development, calibration, out-of-time stress monitoring, and segment analysis. All customer names and identifiable address details were strictly excluded prior to modeling.

---

## 2. UK Company Insolvency Statistics
- **Publisher:** The Insolvency Service (Executive Agency of the UK Department for Business and Trade)
- **Portal:** [GOV.UK Company Insolvency Statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/company-insolvency-statistics)
- **Dataset:** Long-Run Company Insolvency Series for England & Wales (2000–2024)
- **Licence:** [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/)
- **Usage:** Used for macroeconomic benchmarking and corporate liquidation trend comparison across economic cycles.
