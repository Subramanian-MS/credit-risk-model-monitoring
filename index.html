Commercial Credit Risk Modelling & Monitoring

Commercial Credit Scorecard & Out-of-Time Crisis Monitoring

An empirical credit scoring and model governance platform trained on 604,786 business loans (FY2000–FY2009) featuring Weight of Evidence (WoE) scorecard scaling, Challenger Gradient Boosting comparison, Population Stability Index (PSI) tracking through the 2008 Financial Crisis, and UK Insolvency Service macroeconomic benchmarking.

💡 How to Use & Navigate This Platform (What Each Tab Shows You)
This platform demonstrates how commercial banks (like Lloyds BCB/CIB) evaluate business loans and monitor model stability over time:
1. Loan Decision Checker Change loan details (term, amount, industry) on the left to see the bank's automated decision (Approve, Refer, Decline) and credit score.
2. Scorecard Explainability See the exact points awarded or deducted for each factor, satisfying PRA adverse action rules so customers know why they were scored.
3. Model Performance View the scorecard's discrimination accuracy (AUC 0.79 / Gini 0.58) benchmarked against a modern Gradient Boosting challenger.
4. Crisis Monitoring (PSI / CSI) See how the model held up over 10 years and detected the 2007–2008 Financial Crisis, triggering Red alerts as defaults jumped to 37%.
5. Portfolio Segments Compare real commercial default rates across 11 industries (Restaurants, Retail, Tech, Healthcare) before and during the crisis.
6. UK Macro Context Examine official UK company insolvency statistics (2000–2024) showing the parallel corporate liquidation surge during economic downturns.

Data Governance, Provenance & Validation
  • Commercial Loan Dataset: U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) 7(a) FOIA Loan Program (data.sba.gov), containing 604,786 closed business loan facilities originated FY2000–FY2009.
  • UK Macroeconomic Dataset: The Insolvency Service (GOV.UK) long-run company insolvency statistics covering England & Wales (2000–2024).
  • Information Governance: All data is public domain or licensed under Open Government Licence v3.0. All customer names and address identifiers were excluded. Post-origination target leakage fields (such as liquidation collateral flags) were strictly filtered out.
  • Automated Reconciliation: All model coefficients, scorecard points, PSI indices, and decile distributions are validated against independent Python calculation scripts with zero manual editing.
